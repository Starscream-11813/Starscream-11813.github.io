{%- comment -%} The floating glass nav (glass feature 7: _sass/_glass-nav.scss and assets/js/glass-nav.js). On a long page, once the masthead has scrolled away, scrolling back up brings down a frosted pill with the site's sections and a way back to the top; scrolling on tucks it away. A glass puck marks the current section and flows to whichever one is pointed at. It repeats the masthead's menu, so while tucked away it is inert, and it stays hidden without JavaScript. {%- endcomment -%}
